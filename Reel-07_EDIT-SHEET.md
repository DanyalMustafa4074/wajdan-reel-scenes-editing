## 4) PACING · MUSIC · TRANSITIONS

- **Transitions:** hard cuts on the voiceover beat. No swooshes. Optional: a fast paper-slide
  only at the two section breaks (Student 01 → Tease, Tease → Student 02).
- **Music:** one continuous soft piano/emotional bed. Light and hopeful in the hook; subtly
  tense/cold through Scene 8–10; warm and rising through Scene 12–18; gentle resolve on Scene 19;
  steady on the CTA. Per the script: do NOT break the rhythm at the tease — keep music running.
- **Mood curve to protect:** bright → cold/lonely (Student 01 low at Scene 10) → warm/rising
  (Student 02) → calm truth → friendly CTA.

  "It was the plan." breathe with a beat of near-silence.
