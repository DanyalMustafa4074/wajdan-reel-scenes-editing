# Study Prime — House Style (locked)

## Canvas
- 1080 × 1440 px (3:4), rendered at deviceScaleFactor 2 → 2160 × 2880 output.
- Hook slides (Slide 1) are also 3:4 (user builds at ~896×1200 or larger — any 3:4 posts fine).

## Colors
- Navy (primary text, 3D extrude, dark bars): `#17264D`
- Gold (pop-word, badges, highlights, accents): `#C9A233`
- Cream background: `#F4ECD8` → `#EBE0C6` (subtle radial), faint dot grain.

## Typography
- **Headline:** Anton (heavy condensed display), UPPERCASE, two lines:
  - line 1 = navy word(s), ~100px
  - line 2 = the gold pop-word, ~150px, with a **navy 3D block extrude** (stacked shadows) + thin
    navy stroke. Keep gold words short enough to sit on one line (nowrap).
- **Body:** Bricolage Grotesque Bold (distinctive, rounded, very legible). Never a plain system sans
  — the whole point of the brand ask was a characterful font that still reads at a glance.
- **Kicker:** small gold uppercase label, 5px letter-spacing, top-left (the carousel's topic).

## Highlights
- In body copy, wrap the key phrase in `**double asterisks**` → renders gold with a navy hairline.
  Use it to make ONE idea pop per line; don't highlight whole sentences.

## Layout anatomy (inner slides)
- Kicker top-left; optional emoji accent top-right (🚩 red-flags, ✅ green-flags, etc.).
- Headline block below kicker.
- Body region below the headline: one of three types —
  - **stack** — a few short lines (narrative). Classes: `l` (normal), `q` (bigger quote), `sm`
    (muted sub), `chain` (gold Anton chain like `GOAL → PATHWAY → COURSE → COUNTRY`).
  - **list** — numbered gold circle badges + text; optional `note` line under it (gold).
  - **cta** — 3 icon rows (🔖/↗/💬) + a navy "1-1 CONSULTATION — LINK IN BIO" bar.
- Footer: `studyprime.pk` (left) · progress dots (center, active = current slide) · `SWIPE →`
  (content) or `↗ SHARE` (CTA) on the right.

## Why these choices
High navy/gold contrast on cream is thumb-stopping in-feed and matches the hook slides, so the set
reads as one series. The condensed headline + one gold pop-word keeps a single idea per slide. The
bubbly body font keeps it human, not corporate — fitting a brand whose whole pitch is honest,
plain-spoken guidance.
