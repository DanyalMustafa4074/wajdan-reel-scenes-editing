# StudyPrime — Reel #09 "Australia Backdoor" (Script 08)
## Scene prompts — concept-driven, copy-paste ready

Same system as Reel-06: every scene is ONE clear visual metaphor with short hand-lettered labels and
good/bad contrast (DESATURATED GREY = the hard/Australia-direct/blocked path; warm GREENS + GOLD =
the New Zealand route). 12 scenes.

### LOCKED STYLE BLOCK (prepend to every prompt)
> Hand-drawn doodle illustration, warm whiteboard-explainer / children's-book style. Bold, slightly
> wobbly BLACK INK outlines; fills shaded with soft COLORED-PENCIL / light marker texture (NOT flat
> vector, NOT glossy, NOT 3D). Muted warm pastels; DESATURATED GREY for the blocked / Australia-direct
> / slow things and warm GREENS + GOLD for the New Zealand route / good things. Subtly textured CREAM /
> off-white PAPER background. 16:9. ONE clear visual metaphor with SHORT readable hand-lettered labels.
> Keep the art in the lower two-thirds with clear empty space across the TOP for a caption, side margins.

**Models:** CHARACTER scenes → `imagen-nano-banana-2` + sheet (Student 1 / Good Consultant). KIWI
scenes → `imagen-nano-banana-2` + Kiwi reference. OBJECT scenes → `recraft-v4-1` (no reference).
