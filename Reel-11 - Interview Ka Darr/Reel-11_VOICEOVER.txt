StudyPrime — Reel #11 "Interview Ka Darr" — VOICEOVER ONLY
(AI voice: warm, calm, reassuring, English-led Roman-Urdu mix. This reel talks a nervous student down
off a ledge — steady and kind, no hype, no hard sell. ~150 words ≈ 55–70s.)

Kuch countries mein student visa ke liye interview hota hi nahi. Most Pakistani students ko yeh pata
hi nahi.

Aur yehi darr — interview ka darr — bohot capable students ko peeche kheench leta hai.

Admission ready, funds ready… phir bhi woh apply nahi karte. Sirf ek sawaal dimaag mein chalta rehta
hai — "what if I freeze? What if I get rejected?"

So let me tell you two things.

Number one — New Zealand mein na koi university interview hota hai, na embassy interview. None at all.
Sirf aap ki file aur documents baat karte hain.

Number two — UK mein agar interview hota bhi hai, you get more than one chance. Ek attempt theek na
jaye, darwaza band nahi hota.

So the thing scaring you the most? Ya to woh exist hi nahi karti — ya utni scary nahi jitni aap ne
apne dimaag mein bana rakhi hai.

Darr aksar sirf information ki kami se aata hai. Jab poori picture saamne aa jaye — the fear handles
itself.

— ENDING 1 (REEL): Save this and send it to a friend who's holding back just because of interview
fear. Comment "INFO" — aur main khud personally aap ko batata hoon ke aap ke case mein interview hoga
bhi ya nahi.
