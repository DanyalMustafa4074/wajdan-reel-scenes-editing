# StudyPrime — Reel #11 "Interview Ka Darr"
## Scene prompts — concept-driven (13 scenes, two endings)

Warm reassurance reel about interview fear. **13 scenes.** StudyPrime doodle-on-cream system.
Fear = soft grey shadow / small worry-monster; relief = warm gold + green (info, NZ).

### LOCKED STYLE BLOCK (prepend to every prompt)
> Hand-drawn doodle illustration, warm whiteboard-explainer / children's-book style. Bold, slightly
> wobbly BLACK INK outlines; soft COLORED-PENCIL / light marker texture (NOT flat vector, NOT glossy,
> NOT 3D). Muted warm pastels; soft GREY for the fear/shadow, warm GREENS + GOLD for information /
> New Zealand / relief. Subtly textured CREAM / off-white PAPER background. 16:9. Keep art in the lower
> two-thirds, leave the TOP area empty, comfortable side margins.

### ⚠️ TEXT DISCIPLINE (learned from Reel-13 — follow strictly)
For EVERY prompt, list the exact words allowed with: "The ONLY text in the image is: …". Do NOT use the
words "concept", "metaphor", "caption", or colour-direction words ("desaturated", "warm green") in a way
the model could paint them. Never ask for a "caption" — say "leave the top empty". Specify "one single
person" on solo character scenes so it doesn't render an expression sheet.
