## 2) MAIN BODY — paste this (SC-01 → SC-11)

[energetically] You're ready to go abroad. [beat] The real problem? [concerned] Ammi Abbu maan nahi rahe.

[imitating a worried parent] "Beta… yahin reh kar kuch kar lo." [knowingly] Every convinced student has heard this line.

[confident] So let me give you the things your parents ACTUALLY want to hear. [sincerely] Zabardasti nahi… [softly] just the truth.

[excited] Number one — the degree is GLOBALLY recognised! Whatever you study in the UK or New Zealand… it works EVERYWHERE. [emphatic] It does NOT go to waste.

[upbeat] Number two — you can WORK while you're there. Part-time during your studies… [brightly] and a FULL post-study work visa after! [proudly] Bacha khud ko support karta hai — the child supports himself.

[gentle] Number three… a parent's biggest fear. [slowly, softly] Safety. [warmly] New Zealand is the FOURTH… most peaceful country… in the world. [reassuring] Yeh baat unko sab se zyada sukoon degi.

[building, hopeful] And number four — after the degree, there's a clear P-R pathway too. [emphatic] This isn't just studying — [inspired] it's a whole FUTURE.

[sincere] Parents say no because they're scared. [warmly, slowing down] Jab yeh chaar cheezein samajh aa jayein… [hopeful, smiling] the fear… turns into trust.

## 5) PERFORMANCE MAP (if you prefer to direct a human / re-take)
- **Hook (SC-01–03):** fast, hooky, slightly cheeky on the parent impression.
- **"just the truth":** drop volume, sincere — resets tone before the list.
- **Reasons 1 & 2:** highest energy, bright, pitch up on GLOBALLY / EVERYWHERE / FULL.
- **Reason 3 (safety):** ⬇️ gear-change — slow, low, warm. This contrast is the whole reel. Don't rush.
- **Reason 4:** build back up, hopeful, land "FUTURE" big.
- **Turn ("fear → trust"):** slow, warm, almost a smile in the voice.
- **Endings:** friendly and inviting, not salesy. Ad ending a touch more upbeat.

## 6) PRONUNCIATION — spot-check these Roman-Urdu words (respell if the voice trips)
- sukoon → "su-KOON"  ·  bharosa → "bha-RO-sa"  ·  rukhsat (if used) → "RUKH-sat"
- Ammi → "UM-mee"  ·  Abbu → "UB-boo"  ·  bacha → "BUCH-cha"  ·  zabardasti → "za-bar-DUS-tee"
Tip: generate once, listen, then fix only the words that sound off (ElevenLabs lets you respell
phonetically). Keep the number "P-R" spaced so it says "pee-arr", not "per".
