## 2) STYLE INSTRUCTION (paste as the directive — NOT read aloud)

Read this like a calm, confident expert consultant explaining something important to a business owner —
plain-spoken, warm, never hyped, never a hard sell. Keep a steady, even pace, around 150 words per minute.
Deliver "Same ad spend. Completely different calendar." flat and matter-of-fact, and let it land. Go a
little slower and lower on "Here's the part nobody told you: that's not an ads problem" — that's the
reframe, give it weight. Warm and lean in on "Stay with me — here's the order the smart ones build in."
Then read the six layers with an even rhythm and one clear beat between each — do NOT speed up as the list
goes on; put a full stop before and after "Turned on last. Never first." Slow down, sincere and direct,
on "Miss one of those six… you're funding a leak." Keep the ending call-to-action casual and inviting.

## 3) SCRIPT — MAIN BODY (clean speech, SC-01 → SC-14)

The smartest consultancy in your market has a calendar full of people who actually show up.

Yours is half no-shows and reschedules.

Same ad spend. Completely different calendar.

Here's the part nobody told you: that's not an ads problem.

Any agency can get you clicks. Any agency can fill a form.

The problem is what happens after the click — and for most businesses, there's nothing there.

A page that talks about you instead of them. Nobody answering for six hours. A booking link and a prayer.

Stay with me — here's the order the smart ones build in.

First, the funnel. One path. One destination. One action. Not five links to nowhere.

Then the landing page. Not your website. A page with one job — get the right person to raise their hand,
and let the wrong ones leave.

Then the VSL. A video that does the convincing before you ever speak. So nobody arrives asking what you
actually do.

Then automation and the CRM. The second a form comes in, a confirmation goes out. Reminders fire on their
own. Nothing sits waiting for a human to remember.

Then the ads. Turned on last. Never first.

And last, conversion tracking. Closed clients fed back to Meta — so the algorithm hunts buyers, not form
fillers.

Six pieces. That order.

Most businesses build one and wonder why it leaks.

The smart ones build all six before they spend anything on traffic.

Miss one of those six, and you're not marketing. You're funding a leak.
