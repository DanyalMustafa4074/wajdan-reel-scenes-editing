### LOCKED STYLE BLOCK (prepend to every prompt)
> Hand-drawn doodle illustration, marker / whiteboard-explainer style: thick black ink outlines, soft
> marker / colored-pencil fills, subtly textured CREAM / off-white PAPER background. ORANGE (#E07B00)
> is the "system / good / working" accent (glows, the machine, booked slots); GREY is the "leak / dead
> / grind" accent. 16:9. ONE clear idea with SHORT readable hand-lettered labels. Keep art in the lower
> two-thirds, leave the TOP empty, comfortable side margins.

### ⚠️ TEXT DISCIPLINE + SINGLE-FIGURE (follow strictly)
Every prompt ends with: "The ONLY text in the image is: …". Never use the words concept/metaphor/caption
or colour-direction words as text. On OWNER/SYS scenes say "one single <character>, a normal scene, NOT
an expression sheet, NOT multiple copies, no mood labels".
