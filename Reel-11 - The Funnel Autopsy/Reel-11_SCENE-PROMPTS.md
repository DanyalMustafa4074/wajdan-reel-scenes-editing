# Wajdan — Reel #11 "The Funnel Autopsy"
## Scene prompts — concept-driven (16 scenes, two CTA variations)

Diagnostic/three-leaks reel. **16 scenes.** Wajdan brand: ORANGE `#E07B00` = system/good/working;
GREY = leak/dead/grind. Mostly diagram scenes; Owner + SYS carry the human/mascot beats.

### LOCKED STYLE BLOCK (prepend to every prompt)
> Hand-drawn doodle illustration, marker / whiteboard-explainer style: thick black ink outlines, soft
> marker / colored-pencil fills, subtly textured CREAM / off-white PAPER background. ORANGE (#E07B00)
> is the "system / good / working" accent (glows, the fix, sealed leaks, booked slots); GREY is the
> "leak / dead / grind" accent (the clock, the cracks, dropped leads). 16:9. ONE clear idea with SHORT
> readable hand-lettered labels. Keep art in the lower two-thirds, leave the TOP empty, comfortable
> side margins.

### ⚠️ TEXT DISCIPLINE + SINGLE-FIGURE (follow strictly)
Every prompt ends with: "The ONLY text in the image is: …". Never render the words
concept/metaphor/caption or colour-direction words as text. On OWNER/SYS scenes say "one single
<character>, a normal scene, NOT an expression sheet, NOT multiple copies, no mood labels".

**Models:** OWNER/SYS → `imagen-nano-banana-2` + sheet. OBJECT → `recraft-v4-1`.
FILES: `Characters/Owner.png` · `Characters/SYS-robot.png`.

### 🎬 CONTEXT & WARDROBE (art direction)
- **Character scenes get a real ENVIRONMENT/context** — an actual office, desk, window, shelf, plant,
  props — not a figure floating on blank paper. Context adds story and stops it feeling like clip-art.
- **Pure diagram scenes stay clean** (the leaking funnel, the clock, the bottleneck, auto badges,
  calendars) — a background would fight the labels. Keep those on the cream plate.
- **Wardrobe:** dress professional/aspirational characters SHARP — formal shirt, blazer, or an
  old-money knit sweater. Exception: **the Owner stays casual/tired (grey tee)** on purpose — that
  scruffy "before" look is his locked identity; only his *setting* gets richer, not his clothes.
