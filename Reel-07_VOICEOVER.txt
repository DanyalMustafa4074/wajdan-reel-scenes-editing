StudyPrime — Reel #07 "Two Students, One Plan" — VOICEOVER ONLY (v2)
(AI voice: calm, warm, plain-spoken. Slow down on the bold beats. ~250 words ≈ 85–100s.)

Two students. Same grades. Both went to the UK.
Three years later, their lives look nothing alike.

The first one went to a local, unqualified consultant.
The counselor said the UK is heaven for students.
He finished his degree. Nobody gave him a job.
Now his visa is almost over.
So tonight, he's delivering food on a bicycle in Birmingham.
He sends a little money home, and tells his parents he's doing well.
He's not.

You really don't want to be him.
Stay till the end — I'll show you how to avoid it.

The second one paid the same fee.
But his consultant asked one thing first:
Where do you want your life to be in three years?
Then they built a plan around the answer.
The right university. A clear path to a job. A way to stay.
For him, the priority was the goal — not the country.

Today he has a proper job in the UK.
He sends real money home.
And his parents never had to sell anything.

Same country. Same grades.
One had a plan. One didn't.
That was the whole difference.

Whenever you think about leaving your country with your parents' hard-earned money,
keep your goal clear in your mind first.
Otherwise, you'll end up like the many students who go to the UK and Europe —
and have to come back just a few years later.

Want to make sure no consultant ever does this to you?
Comment the word CHECK — and I'll send you my Student Checklist to run before you make any decision.
