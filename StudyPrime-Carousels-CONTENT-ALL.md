## CAROUSEL 1 — "WHICH COUNTRY?" → The 3-Year Method
================================================================
S1 HOOK (image): WHICH COUNTRY?
S2  Headline: WRONG FIRST / **QUESTION**
    - Everyone starts with "UK? Canada? Australia?"
    - It feels smart. It's how students waste years and lakhs.
S3  Headline: A COUNTRY ISN'T / **A PLAN**
    - Fees, visa rules and job markets shift every year.
    - Pick a place first and your degree can lead nowhere.
S4  Headline: ASK THIS / **INSTEAD**
    - "Where do I want to be in 3 years?" — a job, PR, or home with a real skill.
    - Then work backwards: Goal → Pathway → Course → Country.
    - Country is the LAST box you tick, not the first.
