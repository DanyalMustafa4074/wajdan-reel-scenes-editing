# StudyPrime — Reel #13 "Danyal vs You"
## Scene prompts — concept-driven (14 scenes, two endings)

Comparison / call-out reel. **14 scenes.** StudyPrime doodle-on-cream system. Core device: alternating
**DANYAL** (the warning — DESATURATED GREY, stuck/panicked) vs **YOU** (the smart one — warm GREEN +
GOLD, safe/options-open). Keep Danyal sympathetic, never mocked.

### LOCKED STYLE BLOCK (prepend to every prompt)

> wobbly BLACK INK outlines; soft COLORED-PENCIL / light marker texture (NOT flat vector, NOT glossy,
> NOT 3D). DESATURATED GREY for Danyal's mistakes/stuck moments; warm GREENS + GOLD for the smart / NZ
> / good things. Subtly textured CREAM / off-white PAPER background. 16:9. ONE clear visual metaphor
> with SHORT readable hand-lettered labels. Keep art in the lower two-thirds, caption space top, margins.
