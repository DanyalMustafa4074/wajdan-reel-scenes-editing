> Hand-drawn doodle illustration, warm whiteboard-explainer / children's-book style. Bold, slightly
> wobbly BLACK INK outlines; fills shaded with soft COLORED-PENCIL / light marker texture (NOT flat
> vector, NOT glossy, NOT 3D). Muted warm pastel palette; use DESATURATED GREY for the "wrong path /
> field-first / dead-end / not on the list" things and warm GREEN + GOLD (#C9A24B) for the "New Zealand /
> Green List / PR route / good" things. Background: subtly textured CREAM / off-white PAPER with a RICH
> but soft scene behind the subject (campus, city street, or a New Zealand landscape of rolling green
> hills, mountains + ferns, a small town) — fuller than a bare prop, but kept soft so the labels stay
> readable. 16:9. Build ONE clear visual metaphor with a few labeled props and SHORT readable
> hand-lettered labels. Keep the key art in the lower two-thirds with clear space across the TOP for a
> caption, and comfortable side margins.

### ⚠️ TEXT DISCIPLINE + SINGLE-FIGURE (follow strictly)
Every prompt ends with: "The ONLY text in the image is: …". Never render concept/metaphor/caption or
colour-direction words as text. On character/kiwi scenes say "one single <character>, ONE pose, a normal
scene, NOT an expression sheet, NOT multiple copies, no pose or mood labels".
