## CHARACTER BIBLE + reference-sheet generation prompts

Same locked art style as Reel #07 (`/Character/` sheets): **hand-drawn doodle, thick black
outlines, soft marker / colored-pencil fills, textured off-white paper background, 16:9.**

**Workflow (same as Reel #07):**
1. Generate each **reference sheet** below FIRST (plain white/off-white background, the poses listed).
2. Save them into `./Characters/` with the exact filenames given (these become the "Refs" you attach
   to the scene prompts in `Reel-08_SCENE-PROMPTS.md`).
3. Then generate the scenes, attaching the listed character sheets so faces/clothes stay identical.

**Brand thread:** Wajdan's color is **orange `#E07B00`**. Keep the *characters* naturally colored
(the orange lives in the SYSTEM props, glows, and captions — not on skin/clothes), with ONE small
optional orange accent on Michael (a thin orange smartwatch band) to subtly tie him to the winning side.

## 1) THE OWNER ("YOU")  →  `Characters/Owner.png`

**Design lock:** Male, early 30s, relatable everyman (deliberately generic so any viewer sees
themselves). Slightly messy short black hair, light stubble, tired eyes with faint under-eye shadows.
Wears a plain **heather-grey crew-neck t-shirt** and **dark charcoal jeans**, simple trainers.
No logos. Average build. This is the "before" — always looks a little overwhelmed or worn down.

**SHEET PROMPT (generate this):**
> Hand-drawn doodle style: thick black outlines, soft marker/colored-pencil coloring, plain off-white
> paper-textured background, 16:9. A single-character reference sheet of THE OWNER — a relatable
> early-30s man with messy short black hair, light stubble, tired eyes, wearing a plain heather-grey
> crew-neck t-shirt and dark charcoal jeans. Show him in THREE poses in a row, same face and clothes
> in each: (1) neutral standing, slightly slumped shoulders; (2) stressed and overwhelmed — both
> hands up near his head, frazzled; (3) forced fake smile giving a thumbs-up while his eyes stay
> tired. Keep the three poses evenly spaced, clean white space around them, consistent character
> design across all three.

**Expressions you'll need in scenes:** overwhelmed/frazzled · forced-smile ("booming") ·
defeated/slumped · frustrated (pointing/blaming) · buried/struggling.

## PROP & ICON KIT (keep these consistent everywhere)

Not characters, but recurring doodle objects. Draw them the same each time so the reel reads as one
system. Orange `#E07B00` is the "system / good / Wajdan" accent; grey is the "leaking / dead / grind" accent.

- **Lead** — a small envelope with a tiny person icon on it.
  - **Hot lead** = glowing green/orange outline, a small ✓. **Dead lead** = greyed-out, small ✗ or a tiny tombstone.
- **Ads box** — a rectangle labeled **"ADS"** with the Meta-style infinity swirl and a `$` going in.
- **CRM** — a clean dashboard/table OR a safety-net catching falling envelopes (none dropped).
- **Automation / SYS** — turning gears + the SYS robot; a soft orange glow = "running by itself."
- **Calendar** — a wall/desk calendar; booked slots fill with green **"BOOKED"** tags.
- **Revenue line** — a simple line chart. Flat & grey = the grind; rising & orange = the system.
- **Leaking funnel** — a funnel or pipe with cracks; leads/`$` dripping out the sides.
- **To-do list** — a long, messy, never-ending paper checklist with scribbles (the Owner's world).
- **The System machine** — a tidy connected doodle "machine": ADS → CRM → follow-up → CALENDAR,
  boxes joined by arrows, glowing orange, running on its own (Michael's world).
