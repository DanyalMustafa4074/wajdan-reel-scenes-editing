## 4) PACING · MUSIC · TRANSITIONS

- **Transitions:** hard cuts on the voiceover beat. No swooshes. Optional: one fast paper-slide only at
  the two section breaks (Grind → Tease at Scene 09, Tease → Michael at Scene 10).
- **Music:** one continuous soft, modern, confident bed. Light in the hook; subtly tense/pressured
  through the grind (Scene 03–08); a small lift at the tease (Scene 09); warm and rising with clean,
  gently "systematic" energy through Michael's build (Scene 11–17); steady, grounded on the truth
  (Scene 18); a serious beat on the warning (Scene 19); confident and inviting on the CTA. Do NOT break
  the rhythm at the tease — keep music running.
- **Mood curve to protect:** cold hook → pressured/stuck (You, low at Scene 09) → capable/rising
  (Michael) → grounded truth → direct warning → confident CTA.
- **Voiceover:** AI voice, calm and plain-spoken, slight warmth and authority. Let "It's not." and
  "That's the whole difference." breathe with a beat of near-silence.
