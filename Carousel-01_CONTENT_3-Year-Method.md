## SLIDE 2 — The mistake
Headline: 90% START HERE            (pop: 90%)
Body:
- The first thing almost every student asks:
- "Which country is best — UK? Canada? Australia?"
- It feels smart. It's actually the fastest way to burn 3 years and lakhs of rupees.

## SLIDE 3 — Why it's wrong
Headline: A COUNTRY ISN'T A PLAN     (pop: ISN'T)
Body:
- "Best country" changes every year — fees, visa rules, job markets all shift.
- Chase the trend and you land a degree that leads… nowhere.
- Winners don't start with a place. They start with a picture.

## SLIDE 4 — The right question
Headline: ASK THIS INSTEAD           (pop: THIS)
Body:
- "Where do I want to be in 3 years?"
- A job? PR? Back home with a real skill?
- Get that crystal clear — everything else works backwards from it.

## SLIDE 5 — The method (value)
Headline: WORK BACKWARDS             (pop: BACKWARDS)
Body:
- 1. GOAL — the life you want in 3 yrs (job, city, stay or return)
- 2. PATHWAY — which countries allow it (post-study work, PR route, demand for your field)
- 3. COURSE — the exact degree + uni that feeds that pathway
- Country is the LAST box you tick, not the first.
