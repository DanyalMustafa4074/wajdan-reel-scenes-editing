=== PAUSE / DELIVERY CUES ===
- 0.5s pause after: "...all week." / "Nothing. For two days." / "...with a system behind the form." /
  "The system did." / "...after the form."
- "Nothing. For two days." → flat and final, silence either side. The flatter, the harder it lands.
- Weekend one → even, unhurried, gentle. You're describing a real person, not scoring a point. No
  sarcasm anywhere.
- "Now here's the same weekend — with a system behind the form." → the pivot. Lean in, slightly
  warmer. This is the promise beat.
- Weekend two → same calm pace as weekend one, so the two timelines feel like one story told twice.
  Do NOT speed up or brighten here — that instinct turns it into an ad.
- "Your staff didn't work the weekend. / The system did." → two beats, full stop between. "The system
  did" is the line the reel exists for.
- CTA → confident and inviting, not pushy. Land on "leaking."
- Keep pace calm (~150 wpm).
