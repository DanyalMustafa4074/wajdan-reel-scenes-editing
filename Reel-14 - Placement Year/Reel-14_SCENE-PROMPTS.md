# StudyPrime — Reel #14 "Placement Year — Degree Ke Saath Experience"
## Scene prompts — concept-driven (13 scenes, two endings)

Reassuring UK reel. **13 scenes.** StudyPrime brand: NAVY `#162447` + GOLD `#C9A24B`; **gold + warm
green = the good / placement-year path**, **desaturated GREY = the stuck / no-experience path**. Mix of
one recurring student (Student 2) + concept scenes. Roman-Urdu hand-lettered labels.

### LOCKED STYLE BLOCK (prepend to every prompt)
> Hand-drawn doodle illustration, warm whiteboard-explainer / children's-book style. Bold, slightly
> wobbly BLACK INK outlines; fills shaded with soft COLORED-PENCIL / light marker texture (NOT flat
> vector, NOT glossy, NOT 3D). Muted warm pastel palette; use DESATURATED GREY for the "stuck / no
> experience / fresher / zero" things and warm GOLD (#C9A24B) + soft GREEN for the "placement year /
> experience / job" good things. Background: subtly textured CREAM / off-white PAPER. 16:9. Build ONE
> clear visual metaphor with a few labeled props and SHORT readable hand-lettered labels on the art.
> Keep the art in the lower two-thirds with clear empty space across the TOP for a caption, and
> comfortable side margins.

### ⚠️ TEXT DISCIPLINE + SINGLE-FIGURE (follow strictly)
Every prompt ends with: "The ONLY text in the image is: …". Never render the words
concept/metaphor/caption or colour-direction words as text. On STUDENT/CONSULTANT scenes say "one single
<character>, a normal scene, NOT an expression sheet, NOT multiple copies, no mood labels".

**Models:** STUDENT/CONSULTANT → `imagen-nano-banana-2` + sheet. OBJECT/concept → `recraft-v4-1`.
CHARACTER FILES: `Characters/Student 2.png` (light-blue shirt, glasses) · `Characters/Good Consultant.png`
(beige cardigan).

### 🎬 CONTEXT & WARDROBE (art direction)
- **Character scenes get a real ENVIRONMENT/context** — a campus, a desk, a company office, a hallway,
  a window — not a figure floating on blank paper. Context adds story.
- **Pure concept scenes stay clean** (the timeline, the fresher stamp, the job door) — a background
  would fight the labels. Keep those on the cream plate.
- **Wardrobe:** keep the locked StudyPrime looks — Student 2 in his light-blue collared shirt + glasses,
  Good Consultant in the beige cardigan. Do NOT restyle them; only the setting gets richer.
