## 2) STYLE INSTRUCTION (paste as the directive — NOT read aloud)

Read this like a calm, warm mentor gently reassuring a nervous student — plain-spoken, kind, confident,
never hyped, never a hard sell. This is a Roman-Urdu and English mix; speak it naturally the way a
bilingual Pakistani host would, smooth code-switching, clear pronunciation. Keep an easy, unhurried pace.
Open slow and empathetic on "Degree toh mil jayegi… lekin experience kahan se aayega?" — that is the real
worry, let it sit. Take a small beat of silence just before "Placement year." — that is the relief, the
turn. Say "Padhai bhi, aur asli work experience bhi — dono saath." clearly and warmly — that is the
reveal. Deliver "kaam kiya hua hai?" the natural way a real employer would ask it in an interview. Say
"Aap ek kadam aage se shuru karte ho." with warm, quiet confidence. Keep the ending inviting and gentle.

## 3) SCRIPT — MAIN BODY (clean speech, SC-01 → SC-11)

Degree toh mil jayegi… lekin experience kahan se aayega? Yeh sawaal har student ke dimaag mein hota hai.

Aur yeh darr sach hai. Bohot log degree ke baad bhi wahin atak jate hain — kyunke unke paas paper toh
hai, par kaam ka tajurba nahi.

Lekin UK mein ek option hai jo yeh problem shuru hi nahi hone deta. Placement year.

Iska matlab — aap ki degree ke andar hi ek poora saal real company mein kaam karne ka hota hai. Padhai
bhi, aur asli work experience bhi — dono saath.

To jab aap graduate karte ho, aap fresh nahi hote. Aap ke paas degree bhi hoti hai, aur ek saal ka actual
experience bhi.

Aur yeh experience aksar wahi hota hai jo employer sabse pehle poochta hai — kaam kiya hua hai?

Baaqi log degree le kar zero se shuru karte hain. Aap ek kadam aage se shuru karte ho.

Degree important hai. Lekin degree ke saath experience — that's what actually gets you the job.
